Where It All Began

Zebra Software started in 2011 with small, free programs for Windows. The very first one was PadLock, a simple batch script that hid a folder behind a password. Before long we were writing proper programs in Visual Basic and putting them up for download on our website.

These were the programs that started it all:

  • PadLockSecurity
    Our first program, and the one we kept coming back to. It hid a personal folder on your computer or USB stick behind a password. Version 2.0 came with separate builds for your PC and for flash drives.
  • Text PadText editor
    A Notepad replacement with tabs, so you could have more than one document open, plus support for more file types and your own text and background colours.
  • Z TimerUtility
    Timer, stopwatch and alarm clock in one. When time ran out it could play a sound, open a program or open a website.
  • QuickFolderUtility
    For anyone tired of right-click → New → Folder. Type a name, press Enter, done. No installation needed.
  • Shutdown PanelUtility
    A little desktop panel with big buttons for shut down, restart and log off.
  • Text FlipperFun
    Turned your text ʇxǝʇ ɹnoʎ upside down or mirrored. It also had a "spam" button, which we'll leave unexplained.
  • MultiTasksLauncher
    One window with shortcuts to everything you used: favourite websites, Office 2010 or the whole Adobe CS5 suite.
  • Shuffle 15Game
    The classic sliding number puzzle. Get the numbers in order in as few moves and as little time as possible.
  • Bouncin' Ball BreakoutGame
    Our first real game: a breakout clone with several levels. It never quite left beta.
